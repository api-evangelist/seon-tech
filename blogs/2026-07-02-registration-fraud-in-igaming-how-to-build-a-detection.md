SEON outlines a four-layer detection framework for iGaming registration fraud, combining device fingerprinting, email/phone enrichment, IP analysis, and behavioral signals to catch bonus abusers and multi-accounters early. The post cites that 57% of operators report fraud losses growing faster than revenue.
