SEON examines mule account fraud across five lifecycle stages (registration, verification, account updates, transaction behavior, payout), arguing most fraud teams intervene too late. It emphasizes that account-update activity, where fraudsters swap identities, carries detectable risk signals that comprehensive scoring should catch earlier.
