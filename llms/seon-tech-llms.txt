- [Email API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/email-api): Standalone email enrichment tool to learn about an email address's approximate age, provider, and any connected online profiles.

- [Phone API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/phone-api): Unlock insights into a user's phone number history and social or digital profiles connected to it.
- [IP API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/ip-api): Identify suspicious VPN and proxy connections and pinpoint users with accurate geolocation information.
- [BIN API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/bin-api): Unearth information about a card's bank, issuer, country, type, and validity to counter payment fraud.
- [Geofence API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/geofence-api): Analyze device geolocation against predefined geofences for compliance-ready location data and security insights.


### Data Governance APIs


- [Self Exclusion API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/self-exclusion-api): Enable self-exclusion lists for online gambling operators using email address, phone number, full name, date of birth, user country, and zip fields.
- [Erase API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/erase-api): Erase all data related to one or multiple users by providing email addresses or user IDs, in compliance with data protection regulations.
- [User Actions API](https://docs.seon.io/api-reference/user-actions-api): A security information and event management solution that sends Admin Panel log events between two specified points in time to a defined endpoint.
